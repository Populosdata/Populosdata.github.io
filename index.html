THE PLATFORM

Your OOH campaigns, finally measurable.

Outdoor advertising remains one of the most visible, yet least accountable media channels. Six capabilities, one measurement currency, adapted for the Ghanaian city, its trotros, pedestrian corridors and audience reality.

01

Audience Measurement

No more guessing how many people see your boards. OTS, Reach, GRPs and frequency, measured to global standards and built for Ghana's traffic patterns and audiences.

02

Live Monitoring

Theft, weather and time take their toll on billboards. Field teams verify board condition and creative compliance, so you always know what state your investment is in.

03

SEC% Audience Profiling

Socio-economic classification layered onto every board's audience: not just how many people pass a board, but who they are.

04

Real-Time Dashboards & Reporting

Manage every campaign the moment it's live. A dedicated dashboard tracks board health and performance, with monthly and post-campaign reporting.

05

Industry & Category Intelligence

See how brand categories invest in OOH. Track Share of Presence, board-level exposure comparisons and category concentration across the market.

06

AI-enabled Campaign Planning

The Populos AI Planner weighs reach, budget and board performance across hundreds of boards to recommend the optimal combination.
